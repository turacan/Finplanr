Calculation & rounding

Locking: Locked categories and holdings keep their amounts when you change something else; only unlocked ones balance the change. For each category you choose one rebalancing partner or all flexible categories. With whole euros, rounding can be off by one step.

Reset returns to your saved reset point; “Restore factory defaults” returns to the built-in example and deletes the saved reset point.

Online search: Online search (crypto via CoinGecko, securities via Wikidata, images via Wikimedia Commons) only runs after you allow it; searching the instrument database runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. You can change this under “Privacy settings” in the footer.

Finplanr: a free savings plan calculator

Finplanr turns one monthly savings budget into a clear savings plan. Split your budget across ETFs, precious metals (ETCs), crypto and a savings account, and see at once how many euros go into each holding.

Every category and every holding has a weight. Move a slider and the rest is rebalanced automatically; lock entries to keep them fixed. Inside each category the holdings add up to 100%, and all amounts update when you change the budget.

It is made for savers who already know what they want to buy and need a tidy allocation for the savings plans at their broker or bank. Finplanr is a planning tool: no price feeds, no bank connection, no account. Your plan stays in this browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is Finplanr free?

Yes. There is nothing to sign up for and no fee.

Where is my data stored?

Only in the local storage of your browser. Nothing is stored on a server. Export a JSON backup to move your plan to another device.

Does Finplanr give investment advice?

No. It only calculates planned amounts. The preset holdings are examples: check ISIN and WKN yourself and make your own decisions.

Can I use my own ETFs, coins or accounts?

Yes. In edit mode you can rename holdings, add categories and enter ticker, ISIN, WKN and a logo of your choice.

Does Finplanr place orders or connect to my bank?

No. You set up the actual savings plans yourself at your broker or bank.
