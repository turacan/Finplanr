User guide

Finplanr splits a monthly savings budget across categories and holdings. It is a planning tool and does not place orders.

Basics

  • Budget: Enter the amount you want to save each month. The categories (for example equity ETFs, ETCs (exchange-traded commodities), crypto, savings account (Tagesgeld)) add up to 100%.
  • Weights: Move the sliders to change the shares. The remainder is redistributed automatically across the other entries.
  • Language: The English app is at finplanr.net/, the German one at finplanr.net/de/. Use the DE/EN switch in the top-right corner to move between them; your plan is kept.
  • Whole euros or cents: In Settings, choose whether amounts are rounded to whole euros or include cents.

Example plan and your own plan

  • Example plan: The plan you see at first start is only an example, marked with an “Example plan” chip. It shows how the app works and is not tailored to you.
  • Create my own plan: The button Create my own plan starts an empty plan. Your budget and settings are kept. You can bring the previous plan back with Reset, your reset point or a JSON backup.
  • First category: An empty plan shows a row of type buttons: ETF, Stocks, Crypto, Precious metals (ETC), Savings account and Other. One tap creates the first category and opens the holding search.
  • Add and remove freely: Categories and holdings can be changed, added or removed at any time. The plan is your own.

Adding and editing

Adding works without Edit mode: + Add holding inside each category and + Add category below the list are always visible.

Edit mode

Edit mode is for renaming, colours, symbols, ordering and deleting. In Edit mode you can:

  • change names and identifiers: ticker, ISIN and WKN (German securities number),
  • change logos,
  • reorder categories and holdings by drag & drop (only if you turned off automatic sorting in Settings; by default everything is sorted by size, largest first).

The optional online search (stocks, ETFs, crypto, images) may return matches. In the search dialog you pick the source: "Stocks & ETFs" (built-in catalog, plus Wikidata on "Search", giving ticker, ISIN and, for German ISINs, the WKN) or "Crypto" (CoinGecko). The built-in catalog works locally and needs no permission. For the online search the dialog first asks for your permission and names the providers; without it nothing is sent. The source defaults to the category type (new type "Stocks") and can be switched in any category. Wikidata is not exhaustive: if nothing turns up, try the exact company name, the ticker or the ISIN, or enter the details by hand. Always verify identifiers yourself before setting up a real savings plan.

Locks and balancing partners

  • Lock: A locked entry (category or holding) keeps its value while you move other sliders.
  • Balancing partner: Defines which entries absorb a change. Without partners, the adjustment is spread across all unlocked entries.

Charts

Click the name of a holding to open a TradingView chart panel below its row, showing a daily chart for the ticker. The TradingView widget loads only after that click and your consent: the panel first shows a short notice where you can allow the chart. Only then does it contact TradingView, which receives your IP address. Without consent, no connection is made; you can also open the chart directly on TradingView. You can withdraw consent at any time under "Privacy settings" in the footer. TradingView's notice "Track all markets on TradingView" stays visible below the chart (attribution required by their terms). Crypto data from the online search comes from CoinGecko ("Powered by CoinGecko API"); images from Wikimedia Commons have their own licences (check the file page), and company and coin logos are trademarks of their owners, shown here only for identification.

Logos

For the default assets the app shows logos automatically: coin logos for crypto and issuer logos for ETFs. Logos for the common coins and ETF issuers are bundled with the app (no external request). For everything else the app shows a small letter badge (ticker or initials on a coloured background, drawn by your browser, no image and no request); a symbol you enter yourself replaces it. If you want a real logo, pick one yourself via the logo picker (online image search, image URL or upload). If you pick a logo from the internet, the app copies it (up to 150 KB) into your browser's local data where possible, so no later requests go to the other server. If copying fails, the app tells you: the logo is then loaded from its original server on every visit. An image you upload yourself or an icon avoids this entirely.

Logo offer when adding a holding: If you apply a search result that has no logo (not for savings accounts), the dialog switches to a second step, “Choose a logo for …”. If you have allowed online search, it looks for matching images on Wikimedia Commons (for example “Apple logo”) and shows up to 12; pick one only if it matches, or choose Skip (or press Esc) to keep the letter badge. If you have not allowed online search yet, nothing is requested: the step shows the permission box with Allow online search and Skip. Tick “Don’t offer a logo search when adding holdings” to stop the offer; you can turn it on again in Settings (“Offer a logo search when I add a holding”). The “◉ Logo” button in Edit mode works as before.

Rebalance with my current holdings

The optional card below your categories (closed by default; open it with its heading) compares what your holdings are worth today with the target shares of your plan above and calculates what it takes to reach them. Your plan stays the target; the card only reads it and changes nothing in it.

  • Current values: Enter what each holding is worth today in euros, including gains, not what you paid in. Leave a field empty or enter 0 for a holding you have not invested in yet. Your plan above defines the target shares.
  • Counted or not: Each holding and each category has a checkbox. Untick it to leave something out of the comparison, for example a savings account or an emergency fund. Holdings that are not counted are ignored in the calculation and keep their planned amount.
  • The table: For each holding it shows the current value, the share now and the target share. Difference to target is the current value minus the value at the target share, in euros and marked “above” or “below” the target. To reach target, one-time (€) is the amount that one-time payment would add to that holding; holdings above their target get 0. The sum of this column is shown as Needed in total: the one-time amount that, distributed this way, puts every counted holding exactly on its target share.
  • Reaching your target: Optionally add an extra one-time amount (for example a bonus) and choose a horizon: This month only, As soon as possible with my monthly budget (the default) or In N months. For the last two, a month-by-month table shows the calculated amounts per holding; month 1 is this month. A comparison row, Plan (no rebalancing), shows your plan for the same months. Once the target is reached, the amounts equal your plan. If the budget is too small for the chosen N months, Finplanr shows the monthly amount that N months would take.
  • Assumption: The calculation assumes that the values stay as entered (no price changes). It is shown as a highlighted notice on the card. To update it, enter your current values again each month.
  • Downloads: Download table (CSV) saves the current values, shares, difference to target and one-time amounts per holding; Download month-by-month (CSV) saves the amounts per holding for each month until the target is reached; Clear current values deletes your entries after a confirmation.
  • Local only: Everything stays in your browser. The entries are stored together with your plan and are included in the JSON backup. Nothing is sent anywhere. The result is a calculation from your entries, not investment advice.

Export and import

The Export menu has two entries:

  • JSON backup: The complete portfolio including settings and reset point. This is the only file that can be re-imported with Import. Use it to move your plan to another browser or device.
  • CSV table: A read-only spreadsheet export of the current plan for Excel or LibreOffice, with the columns category, holding, ticker, ISIN, WKN, share % and euros per month. It cannot be imported back.

Import accepts JSON backups only.

Reset

  • Reset point: Save your current plan as a personal reset point and return to it later. The Reset button restores exactly this saved reset point; its confirmation shows when it was saved, its budget and number of holdings. Importing a JSON backup also sets the reset point (to the one stored in the file, or to the imported plan).
  • Restore factory defaults: Returns to the built-in example plan and deletes the saved reset point. Create a JSON backup first.
  • Logo offer: Settings: “Offer a logo search when I add a holding” (on by default). The online search itself only runs after your permission.
  • Sorting: By default categories and holdings are kept sorted by amount, largest first (Settings: “Keep sorted by size”). The order updates once you finish a change, never while you drag a slider or type.

Data storage

Your data is stored only in your browser's localStorage. There are no accounts and nothing is stored on the server. Other browsers and devices have their own data, and clearing site data deletes your plan, so back it up regularly as JSON.

The footer of the app links to the legal notice (Impressum) and the privacy policy. Under "Privacy settings" you can allow or withdraw each of the two permissions (TradingView "Charts" and "Online search") at any time; a change applies immediately, also in other open tabs. Finplanr itself sets no cookies of its own and loads nothing from third parties without your permission; technical cookies of the hosting provider's security features may exist, see the privacy policy.

Data sources

The preset instrument data (names, tickers, ISIN, WKN) was checked against Deutsche Börse, Vanguard, WisdomTree, as of 13 Oct 2026. Finplanr uses no price data. Check the ISIN and WKN with your broker or the issuer before you buy.

Note

Securities, commodities and crypto can lose value. Finplanr only calculates planned savings amounts and does not provide investment or tax advice.
